ShowFire Support

ShowFire is a cue player and show controller for live performers, on iPad and Mac: music, video, pictures, a live camera and lighting, run from one Fire button or a presenter remote.

Get help

Email magnusjune.dev@gmail.com with what you were doing, what you expected and what happened. A screenshot helps. Testing a beta? Send feedback from the TestFlight app, which includes your device details.

Purchases

The free version runs a show for 5 minutes each time ShowFire opens. One purchase of the Full Version unlocks ShowFire on every iPad and Mac signed in to the same Apple Account. On a new device, open Settings → About ShowFire and choose Restore Purchase.
