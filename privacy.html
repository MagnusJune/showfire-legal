ShowFire Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

ShowFire doesn't collect, keep or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

ShowFire is a cue player and show controller for iPad and Mac, made by Magnus June. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Your show stays yours

Your setlists, cue settings, lighting looks and settings, and the music, videos and pictures you add, stay on your iPad or Mac. If iCloud is on, ShowFire keeps them in your own iCloud Drive so your devices stay in step. Apple stores that data under your Apple Account; Magnus June can't see it.

Camera

ShowFire can show a camera you connect (or a nearby iPhone, on a Mac) on your TV or projector. The picture is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Your network and equipment

ShowFire talks to equipment you set up yourself: lighting nodes over Art-Net, MIDI devices and computers, and QLab. It sends show commands only to the devices you choose, on your own network. Bluetooth is used only for MIDI.

Purchases

The ShowFire Full Version is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; ShowFire only learns whether your Apple Account has bought it. Magnus June never sees your name, email address or payment details.

Beta testing

If you test ShowFire through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share some information with Magnus June as described in Apple's TestFlight terms, such as crash reports and the feedback you choose to send. It's used only to fix and improve ShowFire.

Children

ShowFire isn't directed at children, and it collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
